01 The Doctrine

Three studies in the dark

Three things
the dark teaches.

Nothing here asks to be believed. It asks to be practiced. The owl does not chase every sound. The blade is not drawn to prove anything. You come back to the same mountain until the climbing becomes the point.

Study 01観

Observe

Before you obey a thought, look at it. Most of the noise is not yours. Sit still long enough to hear the difference.

Practice — the first breath

Study 02断

Cut

A blade is not drawn to impress. It is drawn to remove what has already ended. Say the quiet no before the loud yes.

Practice — the clean severance

Study 03帰

Return

The mountain does not remember you. Climb it again anyway. The way back is not a setback. It is the whole practice.

Practice — the daily ascent
